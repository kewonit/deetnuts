## Sources and licences

Logos come from the institute's website where a clean mark was published, otherwise from the institute's Wikipedia article. Campus photographs come from the institute's website, otherwise from Wikipedia or a Wikimedia Commons search by name. Every Commons match was checked against its file description or category before use. Event banners, collages, people-first photos, architectural renders of unbuilt campuses and photos of other institutions were rejected.

## Provenance and preparation

[The media manifest](college-media.json) is the complete checklist. Each entry keeps the canonical path, college name, official website, attempted source pages, review date, outcome and notes. Each selected asset records its source page and original URL, original and final dimensions, SHA-256 checksums of the original and the served file, crop coordinates when used, its licence and author for Wikimedia files, and the reviewed banner focal point.

Files are served from `public/jee/colleges/<id>/logo.webp` and `campus.webp`. Logos are lossless WebP with the longest edge at most 512px. Photos are WebP quality 80 at most 1600px wide. Small originals are never enlarged. Wikimedia files were fetched as standard 960px or 1280px thumbnails, or as the original file when it was no wider than 1,280px. Original downloads, page captures and contact sheets stay in the ignored `data/output/college-media/jee/` scratch folder.

Banner focal points were reviewed at a 1120×192 crop, wider than the page ever shows, and at the 360×128 mobile crop. Logos were reviewed on the white logo frame used in both themes. IIIT Raichur's white mark keeps its reviewed navy background.

Identical files across institutes are intentional:

- `bit-deoghar`, `bit-mesra`, `bit-patna` (logo)
- `iicpt-thanjavur`, `niftem-thanjavur` (logo)
- `iicpt-thanjavur`, `niftem-thanjavur` (campus)

- `niamt-ranchi`, `nifft-ranchi` (campus)
- `nielit-ajmer`, `nielit-aurangabad`, `nielit-gorakhpur`, `nielit-ropar` (logo)
- `pec-puducherry`, `ptu-puducherry` (logo)

These are one institution under two catalog entries (a rename or a second campus listing), off-campus centres using the parent institute's mark, or centres of one national body. Where a second campus could not be matched to a photograph, it keeps the neutral banner rather than borrowing the parent campus.
